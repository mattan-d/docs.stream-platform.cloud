Automated captions in Zoom

How to turn on Automated captions for the Zoom account, for one user, and during a meeting — including Hebrew as the caption language.

Why this matters for Stream

The setting lives in Zoom, not on the Stream settings page. It shows captions during the meeting. If the cloud recording includes a caption or transcript file, the local_stream plugin stores it and uploads it to Stream together with the video.

Menu names follow the Zoom interface language. The steps below use the English labels, with the Hebrew label in parentheses where it differs.

Turn on for the whole account

For a Zoom account admin:

  1. Sign in at zoom.us.
  2. In the side menu: Account Management → Account Settings (ניהול חשבון → הגדרות חשבון).
  3. Open the Meeting tab (פגישות).
  4. Scroll to In Meeting (Advanced) (בפגישה (מתקדם)).
  5. Turn on Automated captions (כתוביות אוטומטיות).
  6. Click Save if that button is shown. On some accounts the switch is saved immediately.
Lock: the lock next to the setting applies it to every user and stops them from turning it off themselves.

Turn on for one user

If the account admin has not locked the setting, each user can turn it on:

  1. Sign in to Zoom.
  2. Go to Settings → Meeting (הגדרות → פגישה).
  3. Under In Meeting (Advanced), turn on Automated captions.

During the meeting

After the option is available on the account, the host still turns captions on in the meeting:

  1. At the bottom of the Zoom window, click Show Captions or CC (כתוביות).
  2. Choose Enable Automated Captions (הפעל כתוביות אוטומטיות).
  3. Set the caption language to Hebrew when that is the spoken language.

Always show captions

So the Zoom desktop app shows captions in every meeting and webinar, without turning them on again:

  1. Click the profile picture.
  2. Choose Settings (הגדרות).
  3. Open Accessibility (נגישות).
  4. Turn on Always show captions in meetings and webinars (הצג תמיד כתוביות בפגישות ובוובינרים).

This is a setting on that participant’s computer. It does not replace turning the feature on in the Zoom account.

Recording sync itself is described in the Moodle integration guide.
