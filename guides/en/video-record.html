Overview

The Personal recording page lets you record the camera, screen share, or both together. When you stop, the video is saved automatically and appears under My videos. URL: /videos/record (for example https://dev.video/videos/record).

The feature is available only when recording is enabled in system settings, and only for users who are not viewers-only.

Opening the page

In the header, click + Create, then choose Personal recording. You can also open /videos/record directly.

Control dock

At the bottom of the page is a Meet-style dock with these controls:

  • Microphone – mute/unmute, with a volume meter.
  • Camera – turn the camera on or off.
  • Background blur – blur the background behind the camera (when the camera is on).
  • Screen share – choose a window or screen to record.
  • Annotate screen – draw over the preview (see below).
  • Settings – pick camera and microphone devices, and PiP camera corner when both sources are active.
  • Start recording / Stop recording – start, pause/resume, and stop with save.
  • Exit – return to My videos without saving a new recording.
Note: Allow camera and microphone permissions in the browser. Without permission you will see an error and no preview.

Camera and screen share together

You can enable camera and screen share at the same time. Usually the screen is large and the camera appears as a round PiP. In Settings you can choose the camera corner. When you stop, the system combines both streams into one video.

On-screen annotation (pen)

Click Annotate screen (drawing icon). When active, the button shows a colored ring and a color palette appears.

  • Draw on the preview with the mouse or touch.
  • Pick a color from the strip (red, orange, yellow, green, blue, white, black).
  • Click Clear annotations (eraser) to remove all marks.

Annotations are included in the final video — they are composited into the recorded frames while you record.

Recording and saving

  1. Turn on camera and/or screen share until a preview appears.
  2. Click Start recording. You can pause and resume during the take.
  3. Click Stop recording. A saving screen appears while the video is stored.
  4. After save you are redirected to My videos, where you can edit title, privacy, and more.
